# Health Infrastructure Intelligence Platform

## Purpose

The platform transforms raw international development, procurement, and donor data into commercial intelligence for Faram East Africa.

The architecture is designed around reproducible data pipelines, canonical entities, and layered intelligence.

---

# Platform Layers

## Layer 0 – Raw Sources

Examples:

- IATI XML
- OECD CRS
- Procurement portals
- Donor data
- Ministry data

This layer is immutable.

---

## Layer 1 – Normalized Data

Purpose:

Convert raw data into standardized tables.

Examples:

- activities
- budgets
- transactions
- organisations

---

## Layer 2 – Canonical Entities

Purpose:

Represent real-world entities.

Examples:

- organisations
- countries
- manufacturers
- products
- diseases

---

## Layer 3 – Intelligence

Purpose:

Generate insights.

Examples:

- market overview
- procurement intelligence
- opportunity intelligence
- organisation intelligence

---

## Layer 4 – Commercial Intelligence

Purpose:

Support business development.

Examples:

- target accounts
- recommended actions
- CRM
- opportunity ranking

---

## Layer 5 – Prediction

Purpose:

Forecast procurement activity.

Examples:

- procurement prediction
- tender prediction
- funding forecasts
