# Entity Model

## Organisation

A real-world institution.

Examples:

- WHO
- UNICEF
- Ministry of Health Kenya

---

## Alias

A source-specific representation of an organisation.

Example:

UNICEF Regular Resources

↓

UNICEF

---

## Relationship

Connection between two organisations.

Examples:

Parent

Country Office

Regional Office

Partner

Implementer

Funder

---

## Resolution

The process of matching a source organisation to a canonical organisation.
