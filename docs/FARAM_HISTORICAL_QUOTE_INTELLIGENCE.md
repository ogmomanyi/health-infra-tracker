# Faram Historical Quote Intelligence

## Purpose

`data/faram_historical_quote_evidence.csv` records product and manufacturer evidence discovered in Faram mailbox quotation, tender-support, project, partnership and order correspondence.

This dataset is **historical evidence**, not proof of current Faram representation.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. **Current controlled catalogue** — authoritative for current product/principal/territory status.
2. **Historical quotation/commercial evidence** — establishes previous evaluation, pricing, tender support, partnership or order activity.
3. **External procurement evidence** — establishes market demand, but does not establish Faram capability or representation.
4. **Generic market knowledge** — may support taxonomy only; it must not create a Faram relationship.

## Outputs

- `data/faram_historical_quote_evidence.csv` — evidence-level records seeded from Outlook research.
- `data/faram_historical_quote_summary.csv` — grouped product/manufacturer history generated by `procurement_intelligence.historical_quote_intelligence`.

## Familiarity scoring

The historical familiarity layer is additive and capped at **10 points** per opportunity.

Evidence is strongest when an opportunity explicitly matches a historical model or product, followed by explicit manufacturer evidence and then product-family evidence. Repeated historical records add a small capped bonus.

Historical familiarity must never override current catalogue, principal-status or territory controls.

## Data quality rules

- Preserve the source email ID whenever available.
- Do not invent missing models, manufacturers or principal status.
- A product mentioned in an external procurement request is not automatically a Faram historical quote.
- Supplier/manufacturer identity comes from mailbox evidence itself.
- Duplicate email threads should eventually be consolidated into evidence counts rather than repeated catalogue records.
- Old or inactive relationships remain historical unless confirmed by the current Faram catalogue.
- Competitive/reference products remain distinguishable from Faram historical commercial evidence.

## Commercial model

The eventual opportunity logic distinguishes:

`CURRENT_CATALOGUE_MATCH` > `HISTORICAL_QUOTE_MATCH` > `PRODUCT_FAMILY_ONLY` > `UNMATCHED`

Historical familiarity improves prioritisation and confidence. It does **not** automatically make an opportunity actionable.

## Target flow

`Tender -> Product family -> Current Faram catalogue + Historical familiarity -> Principal -> Territory fit -> Competitor evidence -> Opportunity score -> Recommended action`
